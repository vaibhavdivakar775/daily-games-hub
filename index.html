        How are points worked out?
        
    Every game turns your score into 0–100 points, so a typical day is about 60–70 and a great day is 90–100. Your total is those points added up, so it doesn't change when others play later.
    
        
    🔥 Streaks count days in a row you've played at least one game. ⭐ New best means you beat your best earlier score in that game. Both count every score ever saved.
